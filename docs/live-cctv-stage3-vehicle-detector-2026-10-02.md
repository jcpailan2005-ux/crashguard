## 4. Proposal vs. Acceptance Separation & Failure Categorization

Stage 3 independently evaluates the four required failure categories:

1. **NO DETECTOR PROPOSAL:** YOLO neural network produces no bounding box proposal meeting the 0.25 proposal cutoff after NMS.
2. **RAW PROPOSAL (Sub-threshold):** Weak proposals exist between 0.05 and 0.39, detectable only via diagnostic lowered thresholds, but are rejected before downstream use.
3. **ACCEPTANCE REJECTION:** A proposal is generated by YOLO (>= 0.25), but is discarded by `extract_vehicle_boxes_from_results` due to:
   - `below_vehicle_confidence` (< 0.40)
   - `class_not_vehicle` (non-vehicle COCO class)
   - `invalid_bbox` (degenerate or out-of-bounds geometry)
   - `roi_exclusion` (centroid outside configured ROI)
4. **TRACK LOSS:** Loss of continuity after an accepted vehicle identity has been established. (In the current baseline, track loss cannot occur because zero accepted observations exist to seed a track).

  - Paired-sample under 2 PyTorch threads / 1 OpenCV thread: **433.5 ms mean**, **438.2 ms median**, **478.3 ms max** (~2.3 FPS)
  - Full-frame 0.05 fresh pass (88 frames): **857.8 ms mean**
- **Cadence Relationship:** The live RTSP worker ingests 15 FPS native video (Source B recorded at 11.6 FPS). The detector pipeline processes at ~2.3 to 9.3 FPS on CPU. Because the capture loop and analysis loop are decoupled (Stage 1), capture FPS remains smooth, but inference cadence requires high detection quality per evaluated frame.

## 8. Domain Mismatch as the Primary Blocker

The root cause of vehicle detector failure is the acute domain mismatch between generic COCO training weights and physical Tapo CCTV screen replays:
1. **Low Contrast & Night Lighting:** Strong glare from headlights, reflections, and dark roadway surfaces.
2. **Replay Degradation:** Moire patterns, scan lines, perspective warping, and motion blur inherent in monitor recaptures.
3. **Scale & Aspect Ratio:** Distant approaching motorcycles and compact cars occupy small pixel footprints (A: ~100–191×99–183 px; B: ~90–132×72–81 px in 1280×720 native; halved at 640 input).
4. **Failure of Global Threshold Lowering:** Lowering acceptance from 0.40 to 0.10 or 0.05 causes an explosion of unrelated vehicle proposals (+479 proposals across 41 frames) and false positive detections on road clutter, while still failing to establish accepted participant continuity.
